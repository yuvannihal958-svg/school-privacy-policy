Privacy Policy for School Management 3D Pro

Effective Date: October 30, 2026 • Application ID: com.k.y.s.harsha958.School

Overview: School Management 3D Pro is an educational institution management and communication platform designed to coordinate academic rosters, attendance, homework, timetables, and teacher-led virtual classes. We handle educator and student data strictly to deliver these school operations. We do not sell personal information, and we do not serve advertisements.

1. Information We Collect and Process

Our application collects and processes information necessary to maintain school operational records as provided by educational institutions:

  • User Profiles & Authentication: Full name, institutional username, role assignment (Principal, Class Teacher, Subject Teacher, Student, Bus Driver), assigned school ID, and grade or class cohort (e.g., "10th A").
  • Academic Management Records: Daily attendance logs, homework assignments, student homework text or attachment submissions, examination grade reports, and classroom schedules.
  • School Media & Learning Resources: Celebration photos, educational PDF study materials, and instructional documents uploaded by authorized school staff.
  • Device Notification Tokens: Encrypted mobile push notification tokens to deliver urgent classroom notices, homework alerts, and live class announcements.

2. Google Meet Integration & Google User Data

School Management 3D Pro offers an optional Google Meet integration enabling authorized educators to schedule and host virtual classroom sessions.

A. OAuth Scopes Requested

When an educator chooses to connect their Google Account, the application requests access to a single, specific scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/meetings.space.created — Permits the application to create and configure new Google Meet conference spaces for online classes.

B. Token Encryption and Storage Architecture

We apply rigorous cryptographic protections to protect educator Google credentials:

  • AES-256-GCM Encryption: Google OAuth refresh tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM (Authenticated Encryption with Associated Data) with a unique 96-bit random initialization vector (IV) and a 128-bit authentication tag before being saved.
  • Restricted Vault Storage: Encrypted tokens are stored exclusively in an isolated, private database schema (private.teacher_google_tokens). Access is strictly restricted to authenticated server-side Edge Functions with database privileges; client applications and public APIs cannot read this table.
  • Ephemeral Access Tokens: Short-lived Google access tokens are held in volatile server memory solely during the API call to generate a meeting space and are immediately discarded.
  • Google Account Email Disclosure: Upon successful OAuth authorization, the educator's Google email address is retrieved via Google's userinfo endpoint (https://www.googleapis.com/oauth2/v2/userinfo) and saved alongside the encrypted token record solely to confirm and display the connected account on the teacher dashboard. It is never used for advertising, tracking, or unsolicited communication.

C. Student Participation

Students do not authenticate with Google through our application. Students view only the meeting title, scheduled time, and meeting URL generated by their teacher, and join the session directly via their device's native Google Meet application or web browser.

D. What We Do NOT Collect or Access

Our integration adheres to the principle of least privilege. School Management 3D Pro does not request, access, or store:

  • Personal Gmail messages or email attachments.
  • Google Drive files, documents, or spreadsheets.
  • Google Calendar personal events or appointments.
  • Live audio streams, video recordings, or chat logs from Google Meet sessions.
Google API Limited Use Disclosure:
School Management 3D Pro's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Purpose and Legal Grounds for Processing

All data processing is conducted solely to support educational institution operations:

Data Category Operational Purpose Access Restrictions
Student Profiles & Grades Classroom record-keeping and student progress reports Enrolled student, assigned teachers, and school principal
Homework & Assignments Curriculum delivery, submission, and grading Enrolled class cohort and assigning teacher
Google Meet Tokens Generating live virtual class links on behalf of teachers Isolated server-side encryption vault (AES-256-GCM)
Attendance Logs Tracking daily student presence and reporting to guardians Assigned class teacher and school administration

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Subprocessors

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize student, parent, or educator personal information. Data is shared exclusively with our core cloud infrastructure provider under strict operational terms:

  • Supabase Cloud: Provides enterprise database hosting (PostgreSQL), serverless edge compute runtime, and encrypted file storage located in secure cloud data centers. Data in transit is protected using industry-standard HTTPS and modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3). Database records are protected by Row-Level Security (RLS) enforcement.

5. Educational Accounts and Minors

School Management 3D Pro is intended for educational institution environments:

  • Institutional Provisioning: Student accounts cannot be created by public self-registration. All student accounts are provisioned directly by authorized school administrators.
  • Parental and School Authority: The educational institution is responsible for authorizing student access and coordinating necessary permissions in accordance with applicable school policies and local regulations.

6. Data Retention, Revocation, and Deletion

We retain operational records only as long as necessary for educational purposes:

  • Revoking Google Access: Educators may revoke the application's access to their Google Account at any time by visiting Google Account Security Permissions. Once revoked, the application can no longer generate meetings or refresh tokens.
  • Teacher Google Token Removal: Deleting a teacher profile or school record in the application triggers an automated database cascade that permanently removes the associated encrypted tokens from private.teacher_google_tokens.
  • Account Deletion Requests: School administrators can remove student or faculty profiles directly through the administrative interface. Users or parents may also request account or data removal by contacting us at the email below.

7. Security Practices

We implement technical and organizational measures to safeguard school data:

  • All communications between client devices and cloud servers use modern TLS (HTTPS) encryption in transit.
  • Database access is segregated by institutional tenant ID (school_id) and secured by PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS).
  • Sensitive external credentials (including Google OAuth refresh tokens) are encrypted with AES-256-GCM prior to database persistence.
